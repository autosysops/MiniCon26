# Pester 6 is finally here!

**Speaker(s):** Jakub Jares — session on the [MiniCon26](https://synedgy.com/psconfeu-minicon/) agenda.

## Abstract

Pester 6 is finally here, in fact we are already 2 minor releases in. Come join me to see what is new in Pester 6, what are the best new features, and how you can leverage them to make your tests faster, more reliable, and more modern!
