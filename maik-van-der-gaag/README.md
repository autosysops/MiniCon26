## Maik van der Gaag

<img src="https://cdn.sessionize.com/image/c0f3-400o400o1-R1fv3QXyQnPqr2st5dZh4p.jpg" alt="Maik van der Gaag" />

*Head of Technology - Microsoft | Microsoft MVP*

Maik van der Gaag is the Head of Technology - Microsoft at Databalance, an experienced consultancy and MSP company with a strong focus on the Microsoft. He has over 20 years of experience providing architecture, development, training, and design expertise. During this, he has worked in various projects ranging from Cloud Transformations to DevOps implementations.

He loves to share knowledge, is a public speaker, writes blogs, and organizes events. Microsoft has recognized him as a Microsoft Azure MVP.

If you want to learn more about him or his expertise, check out his blog (https://msftplayground.com).
